Frequently Asked Questions

How do cards get created?

Type a word or sentence in your native language, and Maruanki automatically translates it into the language you're learning, complete with audio pronunciation.

How does the review system work?

Cards are reviewed on a 7-step schedule based on the forgetting curve. Once you answer correctly 7 times in a row, a card graduates. Getting an answer wrong resets the streak.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Go to Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad to view, change, or cancel your plan at any time.

How do I delete a word or restore my data?

Swipe left on any word in the list screen to delete it. If you reinstall the app on the same device, your progress is stored locally and tied to your device.
