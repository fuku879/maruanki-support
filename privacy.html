Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Maruanki ("the app") is a flashcard app that helps you learn languages using AI-powered translation. This page explains what information the app handles and how.

Information We Collect

Maruanki does not require an account, and does not collect your name, contact details, or any other directly identifying information.

  • Content you enter — the words and sentences you type to create flashcards. This text is sent to a translation service (see "Third-Party Services" below) in order to generate the translated card, and is stored locally on your device.
  • App usage data — things like your selected languages, review progress, and how many cards you've created this month. This is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us.
  • Subscription status — if you purchase a subscription, Apple (or Google, on Android) processes the payment and shares your subscription status with the app so it can unlock the relevant features. We do not receive your payment details (card number, etc.) — these are handled entirely by Apple/Google.

Third-Party Services

To generate translations, the text you enter is sent to:

  • Google Cloud Translation API — processes the text you enter to return a translation. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Audio pronunciation is generated using your device's built-in text-to-speech engine and does not involve sending data to any external service.

Data Storage

Your flashcards, progress, and settings are stored locally on your device. If you delete the app, this data is removed. We do not currently operate a server that stores your personal flashcard content.

Advertising

Maruanki does not display third-party advertisements and does not share your data with ad networks.

Children's Privacy

Maruanki is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

You can delete individual flashcards at any time from within the app (swipe left on the word list screen). Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled at any time via your device's App Store or Google Play account settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
